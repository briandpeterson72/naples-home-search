About the data

Highways, grocery stores, hospitals, Walmart, Costco, Sam's Club and Amazon sites come from OpenStreetMap. Amazon doesn't publish delivery areas, and distance to a facility doesn't predict service, so Amazon isn't a map condition: check each shortlisted ZIP code on Amazon. Flood zones are FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer, where high risk means a Special Flood Hazard Area (A, AE, AH, AO or VE). Evacuation zones come from the Florida Division of Emergency Management's "Know Your Zone" map, with Lee County's own map as a backup. Distances are straight-line, not driving distance, and the grid resolution is about 150 m. Before you buy, confirm a specific property on the county's evacuation-zone lookup and FEMA's flood map.
